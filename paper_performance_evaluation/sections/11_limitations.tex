\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Environments and budget}
Evidence covers four closed-API providers and four reasoning datasets at one nominal budget
$B{=}6$, with $15/16$ cells completed and Fireworks$\times$GPQA protocol-blocked
(Section~\ref{sec:setup}). Aggregate identical-pool tests use $n{=}3394$ paired examples, but
transfer and cell-level policy claims rest on fewer independent environments; StrategyQA is
underpowered for small deltas. Algorithm rankings at other budgets are not claimed. Natural Plan
appears only as a supplementary pilot record.

\paragraph{Baseline fidelity and development adjacency}
L1/S1/TALE rows are closed-API adapters, not official reproductions
(Table~\ref{tab:baseline-fidelity}). The main provenance qualifications are stated in
Section~\ref{sec:setup}; historical replay notes are supplementary.
Metrics assume extractable, normalizable final answers.

\paragraph{Resource telemetry}
Section~\ref{sec:compute} gives the resource-accounting detail. Token/USD fields remain lower
bounds, paid retries are incomplete, and the study does not report FLOP matching or a full
resource-matched matrix. Public-benchmark contamination in proprietary training data cannot be
audited.

\paragraph{Reproducibility and temporal validity}
Released artifacts support aggregate-record verification of reported tables and numerical
invariants (Section~\ref{sec:setup-repro}). They do not guarantee regeneration of identical
proprietary outputs. Closed-API model identifiers do not necessarily provide immutable weight-level
versioning, so provider-side updates may limit exact longitudinal regeneration. Historical component
diagnostics are not ablations of the evaluated protocol.
