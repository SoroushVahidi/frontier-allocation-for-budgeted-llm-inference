\section{Offline Counterfactuals}
\label{sec:ablations}

Frozen-trace counterfactuals isolate attribution questions that aggregate accuracy cannot answer:
whether challenger expansion changes outcomes relative to the incumbent, and whether repair
scoring---orthogonal to allocation---moves commits or external rankings
(Section~\ref{sec:results}). Gate-level transfer evidence remains in
Section~\ref{sec:provider-analysis}; unavailable current-path grids and historical diagnostics are
in Section~\ref{sec:limitations} and \ref{app:historical-components}.

\subsection{Incumbent versus full Frontier}

Using stored incumbent answers from controller metadata, full Frontier beats the incumbent-only
counterfactual in $9/15$ cells and loses in $2/15$ (\ref{app:incumbent}). This is a
discovery-side non-inertness check under stored traces, not a matched-cost ``no expansion'' ablation.

\subsection{Repair scoring counterfactual}

Bounded repair is not credited to Frontier allocation. On the completed 15-cell matrix, Frontier's
scored answer differs from its repair-related field on $151/3394$ rows; forcing the repair field for
Frontier would yield 0 rescues and 68 regressions in the available counterfactual, while a
repair-based external ranking counterfactual flips 0 external winners (\ref{app:repair}).
These outcomes separate scoring convention from selection and accounting; primary tables retain the
historical scoring convention.
