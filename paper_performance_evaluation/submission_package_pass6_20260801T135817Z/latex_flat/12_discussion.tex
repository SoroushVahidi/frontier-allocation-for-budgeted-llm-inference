\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

For settings like the one studied---multi-sample candidate pools, trace-conditioned selectors,
provider transfer, or closed-API resource accounting---the protocol implies four minimum reporting
practices for performance-measurement validity.

\paragraph{Compare nontrivial selectors to identical-pool plurality}
Report accuracy against plurality on the same extracted answers and disclose pool-construction cost
(Sections~\ref{sec:identical-pool} and~\ref{sec:heldout-calibrated-selector}).

\paragraph{Validate failure-mined rules across environments}
Label development-environment cells and report provider-stratified rescue/regression for the
evaluated commit rule (Section~\ref{sec:provider-analysis}).

\paragraph{Report nominal $B$ alongside componentwise $\mathbf{r}$}
Equal nominal budgets need not equalize calls, tokens, latency, or dollars; mark reconstruction
status and lower-bound fields (Section~\ref{sec:compute}).

\paragraph{Separate discovery from selection, and keep blocked cells}
Jointly report $\mathrm{DR}$ and $\mathrm{CSA}$, and retain protocol-blocked cells rather than
imputing scores (Sections~\ref{sec:results} and~\ref{sec:setup}).

The common theme is semantic separation: nominal budgets, pool-construction cost, selector marginal
cost, and measured token/USD fields answer different performance questions.
