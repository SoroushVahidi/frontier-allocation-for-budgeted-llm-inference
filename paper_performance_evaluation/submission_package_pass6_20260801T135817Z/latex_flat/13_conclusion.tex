\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Budgeted closed-API multi-call evaluation can change its defensible interpretation when discovery,
selection, pool construction, and realized resources are conflated. This article contributes an
accounting and attribution protocol for those settings. In comparable multi-sample, selector,
transfer, and closed-service studies, efficiency and selection claims require the right resource
layer and fixed candidate pools when selection is the object of evaluation.
