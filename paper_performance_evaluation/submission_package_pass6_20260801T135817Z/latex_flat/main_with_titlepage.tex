\subsubsection*{Broader Impact Statement}
This work studies evaluation of budgeted LLM reasoning on standard benchmarks. We are not aware of
a direct negative societal impact beyond general considerations for LLM inference research; more
selective or more expensive multi-generator protocols can increase or decrease aggregate compute
depending on deployment choices (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}).

\subsubsection*{Reproducibility Statement}
Frozen per-example records and manifests support deterministic replay of scoring, aggregation,
paired tests, and the reported tables for all 15 completed cells; provenance tiers and known replay
limits are documented in \ref{app:artifact-map}. The package does not guarantee
regeneration of identical proprietary API outputs, and live regeneration may incur API charges. The
submission package is asserted only to the extent documented by its manifests.
